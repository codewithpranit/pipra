## 2. Fish Preparation Asset

* **File**: `pipra-mustard-fish-preparation.jpg`
* **Title**: *Maacher Jhaal — Fish cooked in mustard oil and yellow mustard gravy*
* **Creator / Photographer**: Original upload on Wikimedia Commons
* **Source**: Wikimedia Commons ([https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maacher_Jhaal.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Maacher_Jhaal.jpg))
* **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0) ([https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/))
* **Original Resolution**: 2,428 × 3,237 pixels (Master)
* **Subject**: Traditional freshwater river fish cooked in cold-pressed mustard oil with freshly ground mustard paste (*shorshe bata*), green chillies, and spices in a traditional iron pan (*kadai*).
* **Important Notice**: This photograph illustrates the authentic **traditional mustard-oil cooking process and preparation technique**. The fish depicted is freshwater Pabda (*Ompok* sp.) and is **NOT** Bachwa (*Eutropiichthys vacha*). It must never be labeled or described as Bachwa.
